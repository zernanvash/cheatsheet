## What is Malayalam? (Definition)

Malayalam is one of the 22 official languages of India, spoken mainly in the state of Kerala, Lakshadweep and Mahe (Puducherry).

## How to write in Malayalam?

Malayalam writing is syllabic, that is, each symbol represents a syllable .

The main symbols of Malayalam script include consonants, vowels, diacritics and numerals.

## How to decode Malayalam?

Malayalam is a living indian/dravidian language, still spoken today by several million people, it cannot be decoded, it is translated.

Example: മലയാളം is translated Malayalam

## How to recognize a Malayalam ciphertext? (Identification)

Malayalam is made up of large, rounded symbols, very different from the letters and numbers used in English, which often suggests that it is a coded message.

All references to India, its languages, its peoples, are clues.
