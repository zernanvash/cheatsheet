## What is Thai? (Definition)

Thai is the official language of Thailand.

## How to write in Thai?

The writing of Thai uses an alphasyllabary where each symbol represents a syllable which is ideal for transcribing the tonal system of Thai .

The main symbols of Thai writing include 44 consonants (with their approximate transliteration).

## How to decode Thai?

Thai is a living language, still spoken which cannot be decoded/deciphered, it is translated.

Example: ภาษาไทย is translated Thai

## How to recognize a Thai ciphertext? (Identification)

Thai is composed of complex symbols made of curves and lines, the words are connected with a horizontal bar in the upper position.

Thai symbols being exotic for a user of the French alphabet, they are sometimes diverted to hide a coded message.

All references to Thailand are a clue.
