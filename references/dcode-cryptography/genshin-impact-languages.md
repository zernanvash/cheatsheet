## What is Genshin Impact? (Definition)

Genshin Impact is an open world RPG video game available on PC, consoles and mobile. The game takes place in the world of Teyvat , where players take on the role of travelers who must explore the world, complete quests, battle enemies, and master the elements.

## How to write text like in Genshin Impact?

To write text like in Genshin Impact , use dCode's online translation tools (above) which allow you to imitate the alphabets of the different languages in the game.

The most common language is that of Teyvat script, whose writing/alphabet is almost universal, then come languages inspired by different cultures.

Example: The Abyss language is inspired by Latin, Sumeru for Indian, Deshret for Egyptian, Inazuman for Japanese etc.

## How to translate messages from Genshin Impact?

Translation of Genshin Impact messages can be done using dCode's online translation tools (provided above). The work of fans has translated the languages of the game into the French alphabet.

Also, some panels or dialogues in the game are written in Enochian which is an existing alphabet outside the Genshin Impact universe.

## How to alphabets from Genshin Impact?

Genshin Impact 's language consists of different alphabets, characters, and symbols that represent the languages used in the game.

Players may encounter these languages in dialogues, signs, and other text throughout the game world.

To recognize languages, compare the different glyphs used, these are generally similar to real glyphs (from Indian Sanskrit , Japanese Hiraganas etc.)
