## What is Telugu? (Definition)

Telugu is an Indian language (Dravidian), used in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana regions. It is one of the 22 official languages of India.

## How to write in Telugu?

Telugu writing works by phonemes with an alphasyllabary where each symbol represents a syllable (generally an association of a consonant with a vowel).

The main symbols of Telugu writing include consonants:

## How to decode Telugu?

Telugu is a living language. Telugu is spoken in several parts of India. Telugu does not decode, but translates.

Example: తెలుగు is translated Telugu

## How to recognize a Telugu ciphertext? (Identification)

Telugu characters have a curved appearance, with many curls and curves.

The Telugu symbols being quite different from the usual English/Western letters, in fact, the language is sometimes diverted to suggest a coded message.

All references to India, Andhra Pradesh , Telangana regions, Hyderabad city or Yanam territory are clues.
