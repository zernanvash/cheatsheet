## What is the Birds on a Wire code? (Definition)

The Birds on a Wire cipher is a monoalphabetic substitution cipher using drawings of birds perched on an electrical wire.

Each bird, distinguished by its posture, orientation, or other graphic details, represents a letter of the Latin alphabet (26 letters from A through Z ).

The visual aspect of birds lined up on a taut rope might give it a seemingly innocuous look, reminiscent of steganography, the underlying mechanism remains a symbolic cipher.

## How to decrypt Birds on a Wire cipher?

Decoding consists of performing the reverse operation: replacing each bird with the corresponding letter according to the encryption table.

## How to recognize a Birds on a Wire ciphertext?

As the name suggests the birds on a wire are represented by images of birds on a line, a cable, a rope, a string and adopting different postures.

All references to birds (swallows, chickadees, pigeons, etc.) are clues.
