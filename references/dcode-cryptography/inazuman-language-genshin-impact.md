## What is Inazuman? (Definition)

The Inazuman Language is a fictional writing system used in the game Genshin Impact . It is specific to the Inazuma region, one of the main regions of the game. The symbols and characters of the Inazuman Language are inspired by Japanese culture (they look like hiraganas) and used to write signs, signs and texts in this region.

## How to recognize a Inazuman ciphertext? (Identification)

The Inazuman Language is distinguished by its calligraphy inspired by Japan and Hiraganas (Japanese syllabic characters).

Any clue related to the Genshin Impact video game (especially from the Inazuma region) is to be taken into consideration.
