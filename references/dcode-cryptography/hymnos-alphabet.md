## How to decrypt Hymnos alphabet?

The first step is the transcription of the Hymnos symbols into letters.

If the message is not intelligible, it may be written in Hymnos language, of which there are some unofficial dictionaries.

## How to recognize a Hymnos alphabet ciphertext?

The Hymnos symbols are very complicated, based on circles and arcs of circles.

All references to the universe of Ar Tonelico or its author Akira Tsuchiya are clues.
