## What is the LSPK90?

The LSPK90H encryption is a 1337 5p34k ( leet speak ) variant that consists of writing with conventional characters a set of other characters representing these characters rotated at 90° (clockwise rotation of 90 degrees).

## How to encrypt using LSPK90CW cipher?

Replace each character (usually letter or number) with a set of other characters supposed to represent the first by rotation.

Example: >- LL| [/] is the LPSK90-H spelling of the word Y E S (bend your head to be convinced)

The characters used are at the discretion of the sender of the message, as long as it remains readable for a person familiar with LSPK90H .

## How to decrypt LSPK90CW cipher?

Decryption requires separating groups of characters and substitute it with the corresponding letter or digit.

## How to recognize LSPK90CW ciphertext?

The message can nearly be read vertically and with imagination. It is composed of rare characters such as vertical bars (pipes).

This is a polygrammic substitution (1 character is replaced by 1 to n others).

## What are the variants of the LSPK90 cipher?

It is already a variant of the leet speak , but is is possible to use a LSPK90- ACW (anti-clockwise) which would consist of the variant by rotation of -90°.
