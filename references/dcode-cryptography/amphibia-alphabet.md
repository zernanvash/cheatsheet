## What is Amphibia alphabet? (Definition)

The Amphibia alphabet is a fictional writing system, sometimes described as runes, used in the Disney animated series Amphibia .

It is not a new language, but rather a substitute alphabet made up of specific graphic symbols used within the series' universe, particularly by the inhabitants of the city of Newtopia.

## How to encrypt using Amphibia cipher?

Encoding with the Amphibia alphabet involves applying a monoalphabetic substitution cipher. Each uppercase letter of the Latin alphabet (A-Z) is replaced by a unique Amphibian symbol according to a fixed correspondence table:

Spaces and punctuation are generally retained as they are, since they are not part of the Amphibia substitution system.

## How to decrypt Amphibia cipher?

Decoding a message written in the Amphibia alphabet involves applying the reverse substitution: each amphibian symbol is replaced by the corresponding Latin letter.

Example: is translated NEWTOPIA

The series contains a significant number of errors in the on-screen text, the majority of which are typographical errors (misspelled words, transposed letters, etc.). It is difficult to determine whether these errors are intentional (to suggest imperfect handwriting among the inhabitants) or simply due to the production process.

## How to recognize an Amphibia ciphertext? (Identification)

The symbols of the Amphibia alphabet are rounded and are often written in several parts (lifting the pencil is obligatory). The presence of dots or short lines is recurrent but not mandatory.

Any references to the Disney series, such as frogs (amphibians in general) or the town of Newtopia are clues.

In the epilogue of the final episode The Hardest Thing of Amphibia , the Amphibian alphabet decoding chart appears among the documents studied by Sprig.
